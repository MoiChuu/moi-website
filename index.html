第頁翻過的故事 📖

　

⋆ ⌁⌁⌁ ♥ ⌁⌁⌁ ⋆

　

我寫的不是童話，是童話破碎後留下的東西。

有血、有光、有愛，也有掙扎。

我在文海的黑暗裡摸索出口，
一邊碎裂，一邊學著，仍去愛。

如果你喜歡那些「溫柔又殘酷」的故事，
那我們或許剛好有相同的頻率。

✦

I don't write fairy tales—I write the remnants of a broken fairy tale.

Blood, light, love, and struggle.

I search for a way out in the darkness of the literary ocean,
breaking and learning, yet still loving.

If you enjoy stories that are gentle yet cruel,
perhaps we share the same frequency.
